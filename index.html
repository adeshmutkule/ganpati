Welcome to the Divine Celebration

"वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ"

Ganesh Chaturthi, the most beloved festival of India, celebrates the birth of Lord Ganesha — the elephant-headed deity of wisdom, prosperity, and good fortune. For ten magnificent days, devotees across the world come together in devotion, joy, and cultural splendor.

From the grand Sthapana ceremony to the emotional Visarjan, every moment is filled with prayers, aartis, music, and the sweet aroma of modaks. Join us in celebrating the beloved Vighnaharta — the remover of obstacles.

Festival Highlights

Experience the beauty and devotion of each sacred ceremony during the 10-day Ganpati Utsav

Ganpati Sthapana

The sacred installation of Lord Ganesha's idol, welcoming Bappa home with mantras, flowers, and devotion.

Aarti

Soul-stirring morning and evening aartis with diyas, mantras, and bhajans filling the air with devotion.

Cultural Events

Vibrant cultural programs featuring classical dances, bhajans, and performances celebrating our rich heritage.

Visarjan

The emotional farewell ceremony, immersing Bappa in water with the promise — "Pudhchya varshi lavkar ya!"

The Glory of Bappa

Lord Ganesha, the son of Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati, is the most worshipped deity in Hinduism. With his elephant head and large ears, he symbolizes wisdom, listening, and understanding.

  • Remover of all obstacles and difficulties
  • God of wisdom, intellect, and new beginnings
  • Patron of arts and sciences
  • Bringer of prosperity and good fortune

Why We Celebrate Ganesh Chaturthi

The significance behind the most cherished festival of devotion and unity

Remover of Obstacles

Ganesha is Vighnaharta — the one who removes hurdles and paves the way for success and prosperity.

Symbol of Wisdom

The elephant head represents supreme intelligence, memory, and the power of discernment.

Unity & Togetherness

The festival brings communities together, transcending barriers and spreading joy and harmony.

New Beginnings

Ganesh Chaturthi marks auspicious new starts — invoking Bappa's blessings for fresh endeavors.
