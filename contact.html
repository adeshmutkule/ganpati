Become Part of the Divine Celebration

"सर्वे भवन्तु सुखिनः"

Join thousands of devotees in celebrating the grand Ganesh Chaturthi festival. Whether you want to participate in aartis, volunteer for seva, perform at cultural events, or simply be part of the devotion — there's a place for everyone.

  • Participate in daily aartis and pujas
  • Volunteer for community seva and prasad distribution
  • Perform at cultural programs and bhajan sandhya
  • Help with decoration and mandap setup
  • Join the grand visarjan procession
