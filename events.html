Daily Event Schedule

A complete timeline of daily activities during the 10-day Ganpati Utsav

7:00 AM
Morning Aarti & Kakad Aarti

Start the day with the divine morning aarti, followed by Abhishek (holy bath) of the idol.

9:00 AM
Pushpa Puja & Naivedya

Flower offerings and naivedya (food offerings) including fresh modaks and sweets.

1:00 PM
Afternoon Prasad Distribution

Distribution of Mahaprasad to all devotees — a community meal celebrating togetherness.

4:00 PM
Cultural & Kids Activities

Afternoon cultural workshops, kids' activities, and community engagement programs.

6:30 PM
Evening Aarti & Bhajans

The highlight of the day — grand evening aarti with diyas, followed by bhajan sandhya.

9:00 PM
Shej Aarti & Rest

The final aarti of the day before Bappa rests, followed by devotional music and quiet reflection.
